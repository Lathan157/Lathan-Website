Node 1 – Storage

Ryzen 5 3600 · 80GB RAM · RTX 3060
Primary TrueNAS SCALE host, multiple ZFS pools, Plex VM.

Node 2 – Routing

i9-9900K · OPNsense VM (firewall/router), OpenWRT VM (PPPoE). Networking core.

Intel N97 Mini PC · Runs Tailscale, Monit, and Nextcloud. Provides monitoring & diagnostics.

Node 4 – Secondary

Hosts Frigate NVR, Obico AI, and secondary TrueNAS pools.
